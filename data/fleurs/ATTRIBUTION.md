These four unchanged human speech recordings and their reference transcriptions
are a small subset of **FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal
Representations of Speech** (Conneau et al., 2022), released by Google.

Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs
Paper: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12446
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

See `manifest.json` for pinned source revision, split, original archive URL,
member name, source sentence ID, selection rule, reference and audio SHA-256.
The source TSVs are preserved. No audio edits or resampling were applied to the
stored recordings; faster-whisper decodes/resamples internally for inference.
Normalization is applied only for metric calculation, as documented in README.
This project is independent of Google, Amazon and the FLEURS authors.
